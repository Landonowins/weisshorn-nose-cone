\section{Reading the Outputs}
\label{sec:outputs}

\subsection{The output folder}

\begin{lstlisting}[language={}]
output/<study>/
|-- summary.md          the whole sweep in one table, robustness, heating, design
|-- run.log             what the run did
|-- config_used.yaml    every value used, defaults included
|-- tables/             CSV/JSON/TXT results
|-- figures/            one PDF + PNG per plot
|-- cad/                profile CSV, STL and STEP files (mm, x from the tip)
|-- latex/              LaTeX tables and macros (if output.latex)
\end{lstlisting}

\subsection{Tables}

\texttt{trade\_table.csv} (and the table of \texttt{summary.md}) has one row per nose:

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.0cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{13.0cm}@{}}
\toprule
Column & Meaning \\
\midrule
\texttt{shape}, \texttt{fineness} & profile and $L/D$ \\
\texttt{L\_mm}, \texttt{V\_L}, \texttt{S\_m2}, \texttt{mass\_g} & length [mm], volume [L], wetted area [m\textsuperscript{2}], shell + tip mass [g] \\
\texttt{cd\_w\_11} \dots & wave drag at $M = 1.1$, 1.2, 1.4, 1.6 ($A_{\rm ref} = \pi D^2/4$) \\
\texttt{apogee}, \texttt{d\_apogee} & apogee [m] and its change against the baseline nose \\
\texttt{l3U\_mm}, \texttt{bay\_mm} & curved length wide enough for the payload; nose-bay length [mm] \\
\texttt{dSM\_cal} & static-margin change against the baseline [calibres]; negative = less stable \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\noindent Other tables in \texttt{tables/}: \texttt{robustness.csv}, \texttt{catalogue.csv} (every profile at the
design fineness), \texttt{trade\_matrix.csv}, \texttt{bluntness.csv}, \texttt{heating.json}, \texttt{design.csv},
\texttt{calibration.txt} ($k$) and \texttt{sensitivity.txt}. \texttt{cad/} holds the profile CSV, the STL of every
profile and the STL and STEP of the design (the STEP is the file for SpaceClaim/Fluent). \texttt{latex/} holds the
report's tables and macros, to \verb|\input{}| so that the text always matches the results.

\subsection{Plots}
\label{sec:plots}

Each profile keeps the same colour, line style and marker in every plot.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{2.4cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{13.6cm}@{}}
\toprule
Plot & What it shows \\
\midrule
\texttt{profiles} & profiles to scale at the baseline fineness, with the payload radius; tip zoom \\
\texttt{schematics} & how each profile is constructed (shoulder, generator, tip) \\
\texttt{cdw} & wave drag against Mach (CFD points, model line) and against fineness (Figure~\ref{fig:cdw}) \\
\texttt{cp} & wall pressure coefficient along the nose: why a profile has low drag \\
\texttt{validation} & CFD solver checks: Taylor--Maccoll cones, grid convergence \\
\texttt{trajectory} & Mach against time; share of the momentum lost to drag per Mach interval (Figure~\ref{fig:trajectory}) \\
\texttt{breakdown} & drag of the whole vehicle split by component \\
\texttt{trade} & apogee change, payload length, static-margin change against fineness (Figure~\ref{fig:trade}) \\
\texttt{robustness} & apogee of each profile against the reference in every scenario \\
\texttt{bluntness} & wave-drag increase and tip heating against tip radius \\
\texttt{thermal} & shell and tip temperatures, nominal and hot day \\
\texttt{final} & the selected design to scale, with its dimensions (Figure~\ref{fig:final}) \\
\texttt{gallery}, \texttt{renders} & every profile at the design fineness with the payload; 3D views \\
\texttt{fields}, \texttt{grid} & CFD pressure fields around each profile; the CFD grid \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
