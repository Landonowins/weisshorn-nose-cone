\vspace{6pt}
\noindent The report covers the FD scope (profile, length, tip radius, drag, apogee, stability);
structural loads, heating, materials and the mass budget are left to ST.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Drag.} Seven profiles (Section~\ref{sec:catalogue}) were compared with an axisymmetric
Euler solver (validated to $<0.5\,\%$ against Taylor--Maccoll, grid-converged to 0.5\,\%) coupled to a
trajectory model calibrated on the LVO. At $L/D = 4.5$, Von K\'arm\'an has the lowest wave drag at every
computed Mach number and flies \SIrange{18}{22}{m} higher than the power noses, \SIrange{69}{79}{m}
higher than ogive, parabola and LV-Haack, and \SI{132}{m} higher than the cone, first in all seven
robustness scenarios. At $-\SI{143}{m/kg}$, the margin over the ogive is worth about \SI{0.55}{kg}.
\item \textbf{Length.} $L/D = 4.5$ adds \TRADELEN{} over the \SI{720}{mm} LVS length for \SI{62}{mm} of
nose bay, with diminishing returns beyond.
\item \textbf{Tip.} \SI{4.5}{mm} sphere: \BLUNTCOST{} of wave drag (\BLUNTAPOA{} of apogee).
\item \textbf{Integration and stability.} Tangent shoulder; \FINTHREEU{} of the 3U inside the curved
part; static-margin change \FINDSM{} cal.
\item \textbf{Deliverables.} Profile coordinates, STL of all seven designs, STL and STEP of the final
nose, the Euler data set, six recommendations with owners (Section~\ref{sec:recommendations}) and the
alternatives if the priorities change.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Not found in the ERT documentation} (Section~\ref{sec:flags}): a Weisshorn nosecone
requirement, the fin geometry and mass breakdown, and supersonic flight data.
