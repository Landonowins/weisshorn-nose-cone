\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Geometry}} \\

\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Flow}} \\
$V_\infty$ & flight speed, i.e.\ the free-stream velocity seen from the vehicle & m/s \\
$M$, $M_\infty$ & Mach number $V/a$ (local flow speed over local speed of sound); free-stream Mach number $V_\infty/a_\infty$ & -- \\
$M_{\max}$ & maximum Mach number of the flight & -- \\
$a$ & speed of sound $\sqrt{\gamma R_g T}$ & m/s \\
$\gamma$, $R_g$ & ratio of specific heats (1.4 for air); specific gas constant of air (\SI{287}{J/(kg.K)}) & --, J/(kg\,K) \\
$\mu$ & Mach angle, $\sin\mu = 1/M$ & $^\circ$ \\
$p$, $p_\infty$ & static pressure; ambient (free-stream) pressure & Pa \\
$\rho$, $T$ & density (in Table~\ref{tab:profiles} and Figure~\ref{fig:gallery}: radius of the ogive arc); static temperature & kg/m$^3$, K \\
$u$, $v$ & axial and radial velocity components & m/s \\
$E$ & total energy per unit volume, $p/(\gamma-1) + \tfrac12\rho(u^2+v^2)$ & J/m$^3$ \\
$q_\infty$ & free-stream dynamic pressure $\tfrac12\rho_\infty V_\infty^2 = \tfrac12\gamma p_\infty M_\infty^2$ & Pa \\
$C_p$ & pressure coefficient $(p-p_\infty)/q_\infty$ & -- \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Aerodynamic coefficients and flight}} \\
$C_{D,\rm w}$ & nose wave-drag coefficient (reference area $A_b$) & -- \\
$C_D$ & drag coefficient of the whole vehicle & -- \\
$C_{D,f}$, $C_{D,\rm fins}$, $C_{D,\rm base}$, $C_{D,e}$ & skin-friction, fin pressure, base and excrescence drag coefficients & -- \\
$k$ & calibration factor on the drag of everything except the nose & -- \\
$C_N$, $C_{N\alpha}$ & normal-force coefficient; its slope with angle of attack $\mathrm dC_N/\mathrm d\alpha$ & --, rad$^{-1}$ \\
$\alpha$ & angle of attack (angle between the vehicle axis and the velocity) & rad \\
$x_{cp}$, $x_{cg}$ & position of the centre of pressure and of the centre of gravity & m \\
$F_{\rm th}$, $m$, $g_0$ & thrust; vehicle mass; standard gravity \SI{9.81}{m/s^2} & N, kg, m/s$^2$ \\
$I_t$, $I_{sp}$ & total impulse $\int F_{\rm th}\,\mathrm dt$; specific impulse $I_t/(m_p g_0)$ & N\,s, s \\
$m_p$ & propellant mass & kg \\
$z$, $\Delta z$ & altitude; apogee change relative to the baseline nose & m \\
\multicolumn{3}{@{}l}{\textit{Heat transfer}} \\

$T_r$, $r$ & recovery temperature; recovery factor & K, -- \\

$c_p$ & specific heat at constant pressure & J/(kg\,K) \\
Pr, Re$_x$, St & Prandtl number $\mu_v c_p/k_t$; Reynolds number on the running length $x$; Stanton number $h/(\rho u c_p)$ & -- \\
