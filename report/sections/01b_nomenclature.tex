cal: calibre (length in body diameters);
CFD: computational fluid dynamics;
CFL: Courant--Friedrichs--Lewy number;
CG, CP: centre of gravity, of pressure;
DJF, DDF: Design Justification, Definition File;
EuRoC: European Rocketry Challenge;
FD, ST, SE, PL: ERT Flight Dynamics, Structure, Systems Engineering, Payload teams;
GCI: grid convergence index;
HLLC: Harten--Lax--van Leer--Contact Riemann solver;
ISA: International Standard Atmosphere;
LV, LVO, LVS: launch vehicle, Launch Vehicle Overview, Launch Vehicle Sizing;
MUSCL: Monotonic Upstream-centred Scheme for Conservation Laws;
PDR, CDR: Preliminary, Critical Design Review;
RANS: Reynolds-averaged Navier--Stokes;
SSP-RK2: two-stage strong-stability-preserving Runge--Kutta.\par}
