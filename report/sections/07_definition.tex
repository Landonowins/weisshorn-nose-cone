A radius tolerance of $\pm\SI{0.5}{mm}$ moves $C_{D,\rm w}$ by much less than 1\,\%; a step at the
tip/shell joint matters more (a forward-facing step acts as a small blunt face): keep it below
\SI{0.2}{mm}, and forward-facing steps at zero.
