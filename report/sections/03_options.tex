\section{Design Options}
\label{sec:options}

\subsection{Supersonic flow over a nose and wave drag}
\label{sec:physics}

Above $M = 1$ a disturbance only reaches the downstream cone of half-angle
\begin{equation}
\mu = \arcsin\frac{1}{M}
\label{eq:mach}
\end{equation}
($45.6^\circ$ at $M = 1.4$), so the flow meets the nose through a shock, then expands along it. The
overpressure on the forward-facing surface gives the \emph{wave drag}, $C_{D,\rm w} = D_{\rm w}/(q_\infty
A_b)$ with $A_b = \pi R^2$ (Equation~\eqref{eq:cdw}); it appears around $M \approx 0.8$, peaks near
$M = 1$ and is the only drag component set by the nose shape. A blunt tip detaches the shock into a bow
shock with near-stagnation pressure on the tip, so the tip radius must stay small
(Section~\ref{sec:bluntness}). Slender-body theory gives, within a profile family,
\begin{equation}
C_{D,\rm w} \propto \left(\frac{R}{L}\right)^2 \propto \frac{1}{(L/D)^2} ,
\label{eq:fineness}
\end{equation}
and the minimum-drag shapes: Von K\'arm\'an for a given length and base area, LV-Haack for a given length
and volume. The theory gives trends; the Euler runs of Section~\ref{sec:method} give the values.

\subsection{Candidate profiles}
\label{sec:profiles}

The nose is defined by the \textbf{profile}, the \textbf{fineness} $L/D$ and the \textbf{tip bluntness}
$r_n/R$. Seven profiles are compared (Table~\ref{tab:profiles}, Figure~\ref{fig:schematics}); with
$\xi = x/L$ and $\eta = y/R$: the \textbf{cone}; the \textbf{tangent ogive} (arc of radius
$\rho = (R^2+L^2)/(2R)$); the \textbf{parabola} $K' = 1$; the \textbf{power series} $\eta = \xi^n$
($n = \tfrac12$ is blunt by construction, apex radius $R^2/(2L)$; power noses meet the body with a kink
$\arctan(nR/L)$); and the \textbf{Haack series} $C = 0$ (Von K\'arm\'an) and $C = \tfrac13$ (LV-Haack),
both tangent at the shoulder. The elliptical nose and the secant ogive were discarded before computing.

\subsection{Comparison criteria}
\label{sec:criteria}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Wave drag}: set mostly by $L/D$ and the area added near the tip.
\item \textbf{Payload}: fuller profiles reach $r = \SI{77}{mm}$ further forward; the rest of the 3U needs a
cylindrical extension, which counts against the \SI{5.4}{m} cap.
\item \textbf{Stability}: every nose has $C_{N\alpha} = 2$ at $x_{cp} = L - V/A_b$; a fuller nose moves the
CP forward and lowers the static margin $(x_{cp} - x_{cg})/D$.
\item \textbf{Manufacture}: a tangent shoulder avoids a kink at the coupler bond; every option uses a
separate machined tip.
\end{itemize}
