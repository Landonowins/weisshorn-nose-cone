\section{Scripts and data}
\label{app:scripts}

All files are in \texttt{Nose Cone/} next to this report. Python 3.14, NumPy, SciPy, pandas,
Matplotlib; no other dependency.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}lp{10.2cm}@{}}
\toprule
File & Content \\
\midrule
\texttt{scripts/geometry.py} & profile equations, spherical blunting, volume, wetted area, CP, 3U fit \\
\texttt{scripts/euler\_axi.py} & axisymmetric Euler solver (grid generator, HLLC/MUSCL, BCs, drag integration) \\
\texttt{scripts/taylor\_maccoll.py} & exact conical flow, used for validation \\
\texttt{scripts/run\_sweep.py} & parallel sweeps: \texttt{main} (7 shapes $\times$ 4 fineness $\times$ 5 Mach), \texttt{cones}, \texttt{grid}, \texttt{blunt} \\
\texttt{scripts/flight.py} & atmosphere, component drag build-up, point-mass ascent \\
\texttt{scripts/thermal.py} & stagnation and turbulent heating, 1D wall conduction, lumped metal tip \\
\texttt{scripts/analysis.py} & calibration of $k$, trade table (apogee, mass, CP shift, 3U fit) \\
\texttt{scripts/figures.py}, \texttt{figstyle.py} & all figures of this report \\
\texttt{results/euler\_results.csv} & every converged Euler point: shape, $L/D$, $r_n/R$, grid, Mach, $C_{D,\rm w}$ (rebuilt from \texttt{cp/}) \\
\texttt{scripts/flowfield.py} & direct Euler runs at $L/D=4.5$ with flow-field output \\
\texttt{scripts/robustness.py} & ranking under perturbed modelling assumptions \\
\texttt{scripts/cad.py} & STL solids of all designs \\
\texttt{scripts/figures\_extra.py} & design gallery, pressure fields, drag breakdown, robustness \\
\texttt{results/fields/*.npz} & flow fields at $L/D=4.5$, $M=1.4$ and 1.2 \\
\texttt{results/robustness.csv} & apogee of every shape in every scenario \\
\texttt{results/cad/*.stl} & watertight STL solids (mm) of the seven designs and the final nose \\
\texttt{scripts/make\_tables.py} & tables and every number quoted in the text (\texttt{sections/macros.tex}) \\
\texttt{results/euler\_sweep.csv} & run log of completed jobs with iteration counts \\
\texttt{results/cp/*.npz} & wall pressure distributions of every run \\
\texttt{results/trade\_table.csv} & trade-study table (Section~\ref{sec:trade}) \\
\texttt{results/nose\_profile\_final.csv} & final profile coordinates for CAD \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

Reproduce everything with:
\begin{verbatim}
python run_sweep.py main 6 ; python run_sweep.py cones 3
python run_sweep.py fast 7 ; python run_sweep.py fast2 3 ; python flowfield.py
python analysis.py ; python robustness.py ; python make_tables.py
python figures.py ; python figures_extra.py ; python cad.py
\end{verbatim}
