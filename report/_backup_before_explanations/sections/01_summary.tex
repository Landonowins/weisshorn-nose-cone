\vspace{6pt}
\noindent Why:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Drag.} Seven profiles (cone, tangent ogive, parabola, power $\tfrac12$ and
$\tfrac34$, LV-Haack, Von K\'arm\'an; Section~\ref{sec:catalogue}) were compared with an
axisymmetric Euler solver (validated to $<0.5\,\%$ against Taylor--Maccoll, grid-converged to
0.5\,\%) coupled to a trajectory model calibrated on the LVO. At $L/D = 4.5$, Von K\'arm\'an has
the lowest wave drag at every computed Mach number. It flies \SIrange{18}{22}{m} higher than the
power noses, \SIrange{69}{79}{m} higher than ogive, parabola and LV-Haack, and \SI{132}{m}
higher than the cone. It stays first in all seven robustness scenarios. At $-\SI{143}{m/kg}$, the
margin over the ogive is worth about \SI{0.55}{kg} of dry mass.
\item \textbf{Length.} $L/D = 4.5$ adds \TRADELEN{} over the LVS length for \SI{62}{mm} of nose
bay, with diminishing returns beyond that.
\item \textbf{Heating.} $T_0$ peaks at \SI{119}{\celsius} (nominal) and \SI{156}{\celsius}
(hot-day envelope, $M=1.56$). A printed PET-CF tip would exceed its heat-deflection temperature;
the Al tip stays below \TIPAL. The shell inner face reaches \SHELLENV{} in the envelope, close
to the DP490 coupler bond limit, so the thermal requirement must be raised from Firehorn's
\SI{390}{K}/\SI{5}{s}.
\item \textbf{Integration and stability.} The tangent shoulder suits the bonded coupler; \FINTHREEU{}
of the 3U fits inside the curved part. The static margin changes by \FINDSM{} cal, which is
negligible.
\item \textbf{Deliverables.} Profile coordinates, watertight STL solids of all seven designs and
of the final nose, the full Euler data set, ten numbered recommendations with owners
(Section~\ref{sec:recommendations}) and the alternatives to use if the priorities change.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Not found in the ERT documentation} (details in Section~\ref{sec:flags}): Alex
Marseille's nose cone report, any Weisshorn nosecone requirement, the Weisshorn fin geometry and
mass breakdown, and any supersonic flight data.
